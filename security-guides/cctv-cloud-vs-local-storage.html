CCTV Storage Guide

Cloud CCTV vs Local NVR Storage

Which is better for your home, business or farm? Understand cloud recording, NVR/DVR storage, hybrid systems, internet dependency, security, retention and long-term costs before choosing your CCTV architecture.

Cloud CCTV vs Local NVR: the short answer

Cloud CCTV stores video or selected events using an online service, while a local NVR or DVR records footage onto storage installed at the property. Both approaches can work well; the right choice depends on your cameras, internet connection, required retention, budget, security requirements and how you need to retrieve footage.

For many installations, the best answer is not “cloud or NVR”. A hybrid approach can keep important footage locally while providing remote access or off-site backup where it adds value.

Cloud CCTV

With cloud CCTV, cameras or a compatible recorder send video or events to a remote service. Users can typically access cameras through an app or web interface, depending on the manufacturer and subscription.

Local NVR/DVR storage

An NVR normally records IP camera streams to hard drives installed in the recorder. A DVR is commonly used with analogue HD camera systems. Local recording can continue even when the internet connection is unavailable, provided the cameras, recorder and local network remain powered and operational.

Advantages of cloud CCTV

Remote access

Cloud services can make multi-site or mobile access convenient through supported apps and platforms.

Off-site footage

Recordings stored away from the property can remain available if the recorder or site is damaged or stolen.

Managed service

Some services simplify software, account and storage management, depending on the provider.

Cloud storage is particularly attractive where a customer wants straightforward remote access, has multiple sites, or needs selected footage held away from the premises. However, the service terms, retention period, upload requirements, supported cameras and recurring charges should be checked before purchase.

Advantages of local NVR storage

Local recording

Video can be recorded directly to drives at the property rather than relying on continuous cloud upload.

Predictable ownership

Once hardware and suitable storage are installed, ongoing costs can be lower than subscription-heavy designs.

System control

Local NVR systems provide detailed control over cameras, recording schedules, storage and networking.

What happens when the internet goes down?

This is one of the most important questions to ask. A local NVR/DVR system can continue recording on the local network when the internet connection fails, as long as the recorder, cameras, switch and power supply remain operational. Remote viewing from outside the property will normally be unavailable until connectivity returns.

Cloud-dependent recording can be affected differently depending on the product. Some cameras buffer locally, some record through a local hub, and some rely more heavily on an active internet connection. Always check the manufacturer's recording architecture rather than assuming every cloud camera behaves the same way.

Cloud CCTV cost vs local storage

Compare the total cost of ownership, not just the camera price. A local NVR system may require a larger initial investment for the recorder and hard drives. Cloud CCTV may reduce some on-site storage requirements but introduce monthly or annual service charges.

  • Number of cameras and resolution
  • Required recording days
  • Continuous recording vs motion/event recording
  • Storage drive capacity and redundancy
  • Internet upload capacity and data usage
  • Cloud subscription and retention plans
  • Backup requirements
  • Maintenance and replacement costs

How much CCTV storage do you need?

Storage requirements are driven by camera count, resolution, frame rate, compression/bitrate, recording mode and retention period. More cameras and higher bitrates generally require more storage. Continuous recording usually consumes more storage than event-based recording.

Don't choose storage from camera megapixels alone. A proper CCTV storage calculation should consider the actual recording bitrate, number of cameras, recording schedule and desired retention period.

For more detail, see our CCTV Storage & Hard Drive Guide, CCTV Resolution Guide and CCTV FPS Guide.

Which is more secure?

Security depends more on the architecture and configuration than simply choosing cloud or local storage. Local systems should use strong unique passwords, current firmware, restricted user privileges, secure network design and appropriate physical protection. Cloud accounts should use strong credentials, multi-factor authentication where available and careful control of who can access the account.

For IP CCTV, network segmentation can also reduce exposure. Our CCTV VLAN Guide explains how camera networks can be separated from ordinary user devices.

Remote viewing: cloud vs NVR

Modern NVR systems can provide remote viewing through supported applications, so choosing an NVR does not automatically mean giving up phone access. The difference is how the remote service is implemented, what data passes through the vendor's infrastructure, and what happens when the internet connection is unavailable.

Before deploying remote viewing, secure the recorder and network rather than exposing unnecessary services directly to the public internet. See our CCTV Cybersecurity Guide and CCTV Remote Viewing Guide.

Hybrid CCTV: the best of both worlds?

A hybrid design can use local NVR storage as the primary recording platform while sending selected events, clips or backups to a remote service. This can provide local resilience plus an off-site copy for important footage.

  • Keep full-resolution recordings locally where practical.
  • Use remote storage for critical events or selected cameras.
  • Protect the NVR and networking equipment physically.
  • Use UPS-backed power where appropriate.
  • Define who can access recordings and for how long.

Which option suits different properties?

Homes

For a typical home, a local NVR with secure remote viewing can be a practical choice where reliable recording and manageable long-term costs matter. Cloud services may be useful for simple systems or selected event backups.

Businesses

Businesses often benefit from local recording because camera counts and retention requirements can be substantial. Cloud or hybrid storage can add off-site resilience and support multi-site monitoring.

Farms and large properties

Farms may have long cable runs, remote buildings and variable connectivity. Local recording at a central or distributed location, combined with fibre or wireless networking where required, can be more practical than depending entirely on cloud upload.

CCTV storage planning checklist

  • How many cameras will be installed?
  • What resolution and bitrate will each camera use?
  • Will recording be continuous, scheduled or event-based?
  • How many days of footage are required?
  • Is the internet connection reliable enough for the intended cloud workload?
  • What happens to recording when the internet fails?
  • Do you need an off-site copy of important footage?
  • Who should be allowed to view or export recordings?
  • Will the NVR, switch and cameras have backup power?
  • How will old footage be overwritten, archived or deleted?

Frequently asked questions

Is cloud CCTV better than an NVR?

Not automatically. Cloud storage is convenient for remote and off-site access, while an NVR gives strong local control and can continue recording during an internet outage. Hybrid systems can combine the two.

Does CCTV still record if the internet goes down?

A correctly configured local NVR/DVR can continue recording locally when internet service fails. Remote access may be unavailable until connectivity returns.

Is local CCTV storage secure?

Yes, when the recorder and network are properly protected. Use strong passwords, current firmware, appropriate permissions, network security and physical protection.

Can I use cloud and an NVR together?

Yes. Depending on the equipment, local recording can be combined with cloud services or off-site backup for selected footage.
